Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

CarloDS (“the app”) respects your privacy. This policy describes what information the app processes and why.

Data stored on your device

Games, saves, save states, screenshots, cheats and download content remain on your device, in the folder you select or in the app’s private storage. They are not uploaded to us.

The app has no user accounts and does not ask for your name, email address or phone number. The console profile (nickname, birthday, favourite colour and language) is stored only on your device so that games can read it.

Local wireless play

When wireless play is enabled, your device communicates directly with other devices on the same local network (Wi‑Fi or a mobile hotspot), never through our servers. The data exchanged consists of the game data required for multiplayer, the nickname from your console profile and the name of the game being played. The built-in replacement for the discontinued online service also runs on your device and serves only devices on your local network.

Microphone

Some games use a microphone. The app requests microphone permission only when you enable the microphone. Audio is passed directly to the game while you play; it is not recorded, stored or transmitted.

Cloud saves (Google Drive)

Cloud saves are optional. If you enable them, the app requests access only to the files it creates in your Google Drive and transfers your save files directly between your device and your Google Drive. We do not receive these files. You can disconnect Google Drive at any time in Settings.

RetroAchievements

RetroAchievements is optional. If you sign in, your username and sign-in token, an identifier of the game being played and your achievement progress are sent to RetroAchievements, an independent service governed by its own privacy policy.

Problem reports

When you choose to report a problem, the app prepares an email containing your device model, Android version, app version, the game and settings in use, the app’s log and, optionally, a screenshot. Nothing is sent unless you send the email from your email app.

Advertising

The app displays ads through Google AdMob, which may also serve ads through its mediation partner Meta Audience Network. AdMob and its partners may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location, device information and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads. Where consent is required (for example in the EEA and the UK), the app asks for your choice through Google’s consent tool before showing ads, and you can change it later in Settings.

See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Meta Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. After the one-time purchase, the app shows no ads.

Purchases

The one-time purchase is handled by Google Play Billing. Google processes your payment details; the app only learns whether the purchase succeeded. A purchase record signed by Google is kept on your device so that the purchase also works offline. Promo codes are redeemed by Google Play, not by us.

App updates

The app asks Google Play whether a newer version is available. This check is handled by Google Play; no personal information is sent to us.

Crash reports and usage statistics (Google Firebase)

The app uses Google Firebase Crashlytics to send crash reports (where in the app the crash occurred, app and Android versions, device model, the renderer in use and a random installation ID) and Google Analytics for Firebase to collect anonymous usage statistics. These contain no games, saves or personal information. Where consent is required, statistics follow the choice you make in the consent form.

We use this data only to fix problems and improve the app; it is not sold and not used to personalise ads. Google processes it under the Google Privacy Policy and the Firebase privacy information.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated.
