Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

CarloDS ("the app") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

The app itself collects no personal information

  • Your games, saves, save states, screenshots, cheats and downloaded content stay on your phone, in the folder you chose or in the app's private storage. Nothing is uploaded to us.
  • The app has no accounts of its own and never asks for your name, email or phone number. The console profile you can fill in (nickname, birthday, favourite colour, language) is stored only on your phone, for the games to read, as on the original handheld.
  • Download-content templates and cheat files you import or share are read from, or sent to, only the place you pick in Android's file picker or share menu.
  • If you insert a GBA game from RetroGenY into the GBA slot, the game file is read on your phone, from RetroGenY, only after you choose it.
  • The app has no servers of its own. To find and fix crashes, and to see how the app is used, it sends crash reports and anonymous usage statistics to Google Firebase (see below).

Local wireless play

When you turn on wireless play, your phone talks directly to the other phones on the same local network (Wi-Fi or a phone's hotspot), never through our servers. What's exchanged is the data the games need to play together, plus the player nickname set in the app and the name of the game you are playing, so the other players can recognize you. The app's built-in online-service stand-in also runs on your phone and only serves the phones on your local network.

Microphone

Some games use a microphone. The app asks for the microphone permission only when you turn the microphone on in the game menu. The sound goes straight to the game while you play; it is not recorded, stored or sent anywhere.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob, including the ads you choose to watch to unlock the extra features for 30 minutes. AdMob may also fill an ad through its mediation partner Meta Audience Network. AdMob and its partners may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location, device information and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads. Where consent is required (for example in the EEA and the UK), the app asks for your choice through Google's consent tool before showing ads, and you can change it later in Settings.

See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Meta Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. After the one-time purchase, the app shows no ads.

Purchases

The one-time purchase is handled by Google Play Billing. Google processes your payment details; the app only learns whether the purchase succeeded, to unlock features and remove ads. A copy of the purchase record signed by Google is kept on your phone so the purchase still works offline. If you enter a promo code, the app opens Google Play's redeem page with the code; the code is redeemed by Google Play, not by us.

Crash reports and usage statistics (Google Firebase)

The app uses Google Firebase, a Google service for app developers:

  • Crashlytics: when the app crashes, it sends a crash report: where in the app it happened, the app and Android version, the phone model, the renderer in use, and a random installation ID. It contains no games, saves or personal information.
  • Analytics: anonymous usage statistics, such as which renderer is used when a game starts and whether the purchase was made. Game names are not sent. Firebase collects a random app-instance ID and device information (model, Android version, language, approximate country from the IP address). Where consent is required (for example in the EEA and the UK), statistics follow the choice you make in the consent form.

We use this data only to fix problems and improve the app; it is not sold, it is not used to personalize ads, and we do not use it to identify you. Google handles it under the Google Privacy Policy and the Firebase privacy information.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.
